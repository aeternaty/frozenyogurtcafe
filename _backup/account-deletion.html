What Gets Deleted from the App

When you delete your Get Yo account, we remove:

  • Your profile: Name, email, photo, phone number
  • Your preferences: Favorite flavors, location settings
  • Your activity: Reviews, votes, flavor suggestions
  • Everything else: All your personal data from the app

Note about reviews: Your past reviews might stay visible as "Former User" to keep community ratings fair, but they won't be connected to your identity anymore.

! Important: Rewards Program

⚠️ If you joined Ounce&Bounce rewards:

Deleting your Get Yo app account doesn't automatically delete your rewards account. That's managed separately by RewardUp.io, so you'll need to delete it there too.

How to delete your rewards account:

  1. Go to get-yo.member.rewardup.io
  2. Log into your rewards account
  3. Find account settings
  4. Request deletion through their system

Or just email support@rewardup.io and ask them to delete your Ounce&Bounce rewards account.

How Long It Takes

Right Away

Your account is deactivated - you can't log in anymore

Within a Week

Your main data is deleted from our active systems

Within 30 Days

All backups are cleared - everything's gone

⚠️ This Can't Be Undone

Once your Get Yo account is deleted, it's gone forever. All your preferences, favorites, and history will be permanently lost.

Remember: If you joined Ounce&Bounce rewards, you need to separately delete that account from RewardUp.io (see above).

If you want to use Get Yo again later, you'll have to start fresh with a new account.
